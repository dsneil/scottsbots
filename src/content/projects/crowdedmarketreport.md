I wanted a way to easily understand the analysis from Crowded Market Report over time, but
they have 500+ videos, which is far too much to watch and take notes on. They publish daily
macro analysis videos and a weekly report summarizing market positioning and sentiment.

This tool analyzes each video, extracts the key points about the economist's views on
different markets and plots them onto the relevant charts. It gives me a quick way to see how
their views change over time, and a quick reference when making any decisions.

- 500+ videos and 25+ reports read, extracting over 2,500 data points
- Each data point is analyzed for bullish or bearish sentiment and plotted
- Charts are marked with calls and details, to quickly see how views change over time
- Reports are linked and their key takeaways summarized
