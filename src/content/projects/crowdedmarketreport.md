Crowded Market Report publishes weekly reports and market-update videos full of
calls: long here, short there, crowded trade, news failure. This pulls every call
out of two years of reports and videos and checks it against the price that
followed.

- 522 videos and 15 reports read, 2,872 data points extracted
- Each call scored as a hit or miss at 1, 2 and 4 weeks, from next-session open to horizon close
- Price charts with every call marked; click one for the quote and a timestamp link to the video
- Weekly sentiment heatmap by instrument, plus the most bullish and bearish calls right now
