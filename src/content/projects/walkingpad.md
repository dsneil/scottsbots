A web app that connects to a UMAY walking pad over Bluetooth, so a phone or laptop
becomes the remote.

- Big start, pause and stop buttons; speed steps of 0.2 mph from a 2 mph start
- Connects to the treadmill automatically when it's in range
- Saves each finished session with date, duration, average speed and who walked
- Only reachable from my home network
