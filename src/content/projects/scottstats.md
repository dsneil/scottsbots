Realtime custom scanner and charting app focused around curated universes of
stocks and crypto. Developed a simple breakout model used to surface trade
opportunities.

- Curated universes for sectors like AI, trying to capture as many relevant companies as possible. The AI universe is 500+ stocks across 20+ subsectors
- Breakout model follows the general strategy of [Qullamaggie](https://qullamaggie.com), based on analysis of his many livestreams and manually labelled trade setups
